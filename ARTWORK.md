## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: landscape project cover for Ali Raza's Raza Labs AI portfolio.
Art direction: match a premium illustrated NIGHT-TIME 3D STUDIO / personal digital gallery. Deep midnight plum background (#0b0720 to #120a2b), dark purple reflective floor (#1a0f3a), vivid magenta and pink accent lighting (#ff2d8d, #ff4aa3), cool blue-violet rim lighting, a subtle horizontal pink light strip far in the background. A charming meticulously crafted physical hero object displayed on a low square pale-lilac stone pedestal, elegant toy-like proportions, polished tactile ceramic, satin metal and translucent glass. Rich cinematic soft shadows and a restrained magenta bloom. High-end editorial 3D illustration, consistent with a stylized personal portfolio studio, not generic tech stock imagery.
Composition: single coherent scene, wide landscape 3:2 framing. The entire object and pedestal fit inside central 65% of canvas with generous dark breathing room around all sides, camera at three-quarter view. Recognizable at a small card size. No humans or mascot, no other exhibits.
Constraints: no text, letters, numerals, logos, watermark, user interface, screenshots, infographic, diagram, collage or multiple panels. Avoid cream or beige backgrounds, daylight, excessive neon, robot heads and generic brain icons.
Project concept: Rabta — Typed tools for AI integrations.
Subject: Three small rounded pale-lilac ceramic modules linked by curved translucent purple cables with precise pink-metal couplings; one connector is visibly joining the modules.
Accent colors: pink, lilac, violet glass.
